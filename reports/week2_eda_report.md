## Key observations

- **Health** dominates with 8,655 rows (64.0% of the dataset) — plan downsampling or targeted collection in the other domains before training.
- Only 3,137 of 13,519 rows (23.2%) have a Kiswahili translation — English-only rows need translation (team-written kit + Week 3 back-translation) or exclusion from supervised training.
- Missing-translation share is 76.8%; Ekegusii is 100% empty and remains the Week 3 few-shot transfer target.
- 1 rows look code-switched (mixed EN/SW stopwords) — review before training; they may need re-translation or a dedicated handling rule.
- Glossary terms appear in 83 rows (0.6%) — these cultural/institutional terms need consistent handling (see data/glossary.json).
- English rows average 19.3 words (median 17, range 0–83); EN vocabulary is 16,368 types (type-token ratio 0.063).
- Longest sentences come from **Health** (mean 21.2 words) — check the Week 3 subword-segmentation budget against this domain.
